Research

Building instruments to measure the universe

I'm an undergraduate at Cornell University studying physics and mathematics. My work centers on the hardware side of physics — the cryogenic systems and instrumentation that make precise measurements possible, from condensed-matter experiments to astrophysics.

Spring 2024 — Summer 2024
Cornell University

Pressure-Gradient Regulator for Cryogenic Systems

Designed and built a regulator to maintain stable pressure gradients within a cryogenic apparatus — keeping experimental conditions consistent at very low temperatures, where small fluctuations can dominate a measurement. The project spanned mechanical design, control, and hands-on testing of the assembled system.

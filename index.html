About

I'm a sophomore chasing two kinds of light — the kind we model on a chalkboard and the kind that traveled millions of years to land on a camera sensor.

My academic interests sit at the intersection of astrophysics instrumentation and experimental condensed matter: building the hardware and cryogenic systems that let us measure the universe a little more precisely.

Astrophotography is how I stay connected to the sky I study. The universe is vast and quiet, and we rarely stop to look up — this site is my small invitation to do exactly that.
